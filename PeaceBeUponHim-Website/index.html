One place for everyday Islamic resources

Peace Be Upon Him is an Islamic companion application designed to help you reach useful Islamic resources in one convenient place. Read the Quran, browse Hadith, recite daily Duas, count your Dhikr and check prayer times, all from your Android phone.

The app is presented in English with Tamil-language content, including a Tamil translation of the Quran. It is a reading and reminder tool. For questions of religious ruling, please consult qualified scholars.

What you can do in the app

Every feature listed here is part of the app today.

The Quran

Read the Quran in Arabic with a Tamil translation, in a clean, distraction-free reader.

Dua

A library of daily supplications to open whenever you need them.

Dhikr

Remembrances to recite, with a counter to keep track.

After Salah

Names of Allah

The 99 Names of Allah, presented with their meanings.

Nearby Masjids

Discover mosques close to you.

Islamic Lessons & Library

Watch educational lessons and lectures from the Peace Be Upon Him YouTube channel, and find your saved items in My Library.

Installing the app

  1. Tap Download APK and wait for the file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder.
  3. Android may ask you to confirm that you want to install an app from outside the Play Store. Read the prompt, and continue only if you want to install this app.
  4. Open Peace Be Upon Him from your app list.

Privacy

A plain summary of how the app handles your information.

  • No account needed. The core features work without signing up.
  • No personal data collected. The app does not collect personal data.
  • Stored on your device. Bookmarks, reading history and theme preferences are saved locally on your phone.
  • Location is optional. Nearby Masjids asks for permission to use your device location.
  • Third-party content. Some content, such as Quran text and map data, is loaded from outside services, which have their own practices.
